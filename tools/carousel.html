The problem

Small shops answer the same question all day.

ella.rprice?
nina.kprice??
sam.lhow much
jo.pprice pls

Automation tools exist, but most start with an empty canvas of triggers and nodes. A shop owner replying at midnight gives up before she sees it work.

So the brief: from landing page to a working auto-reply in under five minutes.

02 / 07
Decision 01

Start filled in, not empty.

ProblemAn empty builder asks people to design an automation before they’ve seen one work.
DecisionThe form opens with a realistic example. Going live means editing text, not making decisions.
03 / 07
Decision 02

Show it, don’t configure it.

ProblemSettings and the customer’s view live in different places, so mistakes show up in production.
DecisionA live phone preview renders the comment or the DM as you type, and follows whatever you’re editing.
04 / 07
Decision 03

Answer the fear where it starts.

Problem“Log in with Instagram” is exactly where a cautious seller stops: will I get banned? do you get my password?
DecisionThree short promises right next to the button, and the account requirement before it can fail.

Design system

Calm teal. One warm accent.

The category is full of blue-to-purple gradients. Teal carries every action; butter is rationed for “look here”.
