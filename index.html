FAQ

Before you connect your account

Do I have to give you my Instagram password?

No. You connect through Instagram’s official login, so your password never reaches Murmur. You can revoke access from your Instagram settings at any time.

Will my account get blocked or restricted?

Murmur connects only through Instagram’s official API and stays within its sending limits. Replies go out with text variations and a few seconds’ delay, and each person gets one DM per post, so your account never behaves like spam.

I have a personal account. Can I use Murmur?

Your account needs to be professional (Business or Creator). Switching is free and takes about a minute, and there’s a two-minute video on the connect screen.

How many accounts can I connect?

One on Free and Starter, up to three on Pro.

How long does setup take?

Under five minutes from sign-up to your first live auto-reply. We pre-fill an example, so you only swap in your own product.
